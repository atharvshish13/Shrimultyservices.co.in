◆ About Us ◆

14 years of agri-input marketing
and 7 years in this profession

Shri Multy Services is an advertising material provider based in Amravati, Maharashtra. The team brings 14 years of experience in agri-input sales and marketing, including 7 years working in advertising and branding, serving clients across Central India.

Led by founder Tushar Diwan, the team provides customized, end-to-end solutions — from concept and fabrication through installation and field execution — with responsible project management.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and result-driven advertising partner, empowering brands to grow through innovative and impactful marketing solutions.

Our Mission

To deliver creative, reliable and result-oriented advertising services that help clients increase visibility, build brand value and achieve measurable business growth.
